Laterna

Coming soon · Google Play · App Store

Merge capsules and watch your song grow, layer by layer, right on the beat.

In Laterna every capsule is an instrument. Drop it into the jar, merge two of a kind, and its sound plays on the song's next beat. Drums, bass, piano, guitar, voice: every merge adds to the song, until the vinyl brings the finale. No rhythm skills needed; every merge snaps to the beat by itself.

Ways to play

  • Stage: build the longest song before the jar overflows.
  • Jam: no losing, just play.
  • Daily Song: everyone plays the same song, with a new genre, mood and star instrument every day.
  • Stage breaks: quick mini-games now and then. Slice notes, catch them, echo a phrase, tune a string.

More

  • Jazz and electronic songs made fresh on your phone, every hand.
  • Two looks: Night Lamp and Paper Stage.
  • Shapes for colour blindness, reduced motion, a visual beat with the sound off and audio delay calibration.
  • No account needed. Works offline.
